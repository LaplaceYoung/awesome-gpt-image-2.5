## Reverse-engineered prompt notes (batch 1)

The X pages were opened in Ego Lite. Several timelines remained in a loading state, so the following reconstructions use the indexed post text and preview metadata; they are intentionally labeled as inferred rather than original prompts.

### Charlie Guo — stop-motion animation

**Reverse-engineered prompt:** Create a sequence of crisp stop-motion frames featuring a handmade miniature scene, fixed camera and character identity across frames, tactile paper and clay textures, deliberate incremental pose changes, soft studio light, and a clean neutral background. Preserve continuity of props, scale, shadows, and color palette between frames.

### Veee — consistent details across edits

**Reverse-engineered prompt:** Starting from the supplied reference image, make the requested change only. Lock the subject identity, face, silhouette, camera angle, composition, background geometry, lighting direction, and palette. Render a realistic before/after-quality edit with no new objects or accidental typography.

### fal — scoped editing

**Reverse-engineered prompt:** Edit only the specified region of the reference image. Keep the subject, composition, perspective, background, and all unmentioned pixels unchanged. Blend the edit with matching lighting, texture, focus, grain, and edge transitions; output one clean photorealistic image.

### pigeon__s — state-of-the-art visual quality

**Reverse-engineered prompt:** Produce a polished editorial image with precise fine detail, coherent materials, natural light falloff, controlled color grading, and a deliberate focal point. Avoid artifacts, duplicate forms, malformed text, plastic skin, and background clutter; use a production-ready 16:9 composition.

## Reverse-engineered prompt notes (batch 2)

### nicebabycat — image generation showcase

**Source:** https://x.com/nicebabycat/status/2096048951069995324

**Reverse-engineered prompt:** Create a highly polished showcase image from a single concise concept. Establish a clear subject hierarchy, controlled studio or cinematic lighting, realistic material response, sharp focal detail, and a clean supporting background. Keep the composition intentional and free of artifacts or irrelevant objects.

### synthwavedd — visual experiment

**Source:** https://x.com/synthwavedd/status/2095548263743078665

**Reverse-engineered prompt:** Generate an experimental editorial artwork with a strong central motif, layered depth, deliberate negative space, coherent color grading, and tactile surface detail. Maintain crisp edges around the subject while allowing atmospheric texture in the background; no random text or duplicate elements.

### Scott Stts — video frame showcase

**Source:** https://x.com/scottstts/status/2097398553463128512/video/1

**Reverse-engineered prompt:** Design a single cinematic keyframe intended for animation: stable character identity, readable silhouette, consistent costume details, clear foreground/midground/background separation, and lighting that can remain coherent across adjacent frames. Use a 16:9 composition and avoid motion blur on the subject.

### Pankaj Kumar — product visual

**Source:** https://x.com/pankajkumar_dev/status/2090401473675014654

**Reverse-engineered prompt:** Produce a premium product photograph on a minimal set. Center the product, preserve exact proportions and branding, use soft directional key light with controlled fill and contact shadow, realistic materials, and a restrained color palette. No extra logos, warped labels, or unrelated props.

### ViralOps — marketing creative

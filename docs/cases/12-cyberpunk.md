```prompt
A vast dystopian cyberpunk megacity at night, viewed from a dark elevated rooftop terrace.
Extreme vertical scale: countless monumental skyscrapers and narrow megatowers disappearing upward into rain, fog and atmospheric haze. Massive elevated highways and multi-level transport bridges cross the city at different depths.
Several small flying vehicles drift silently between towers.
In the foreground, a single human silhouette stands alone beside a wet metal railing, emphasizing the overwhelming scale of the city. Rain-soaked pavement produces realistic blue-white reflections.
Gigantic building-sized digital screens are integrated into the architecture, including an enormous monochrome blue female face displayed vertically across an entire skyscraper facade.
Visual direction: restrained cyberpunk rather than colorful neon overload, deep midnight blue, graphite black, steel gray, cold cyan illumination, occasional warm amber windows and tiny red navigation lights.
Heavy atmospheric perspective, volumetric mist, rain, industrial steam, dense architectural layers, physically believable materials.
Cinematic ultra-wide establishing shot, approximately 24mm lens, deep focus, low exposure, realistic wet surfaces, high dynamic range, subtle film grain.
16:9. No text. No bright rainbow neon. No cartoon aesthetic.
```

**Reconstruction prompt**

```prompt
Create a collectible sheet of eight vintage U.S. national park postage stamps arranged in a perfectly aligned 4-column x 2-row grid on a pure black background.
Each stamp is a vertical rectangle with authentic cream perforated postage edges and subtle aged paper / screen-print texture.
Unified visual style: 1930s-1950s American national park travel poster, WPA-era illustration, vintage lithographic printing, limited but rich colors, dark ink outlines, slightly weathered paper.
Each stamp must have a large readable park name at the top, smaller "NATIONAL PARK" text underneath, state/location in the upper-right, and a narrow black caption strip along the bottom.
Top row: Yellowstone (WY, MT, ID) Old Faithful — AMERICA'S FIRST NATIONAL PARK; Grand Canyon (ARIZONA) layered canyon — A WORLD OF WONDER; Acadia (MAINE) rocky shoreline and lighthouse — WHERE MOUNTAINS MEET THE SEA; Zion (UTAH) orange-red cliffs — NATURE'S CATHEDRAL.
Bottom row: Glacier (MONTANA) alpine lake — CROWN OF THE CONTINENT; Great Smoky Mountains (TENNESSEE / NORTH CAROLINA) misty ridges — FOREVER WILD; Denali (ALASKA) snow peak and caribou — THE GREAT ONE; Everglades (FLORIDA) sunset wetland and heron — RIVER OF GRASS.
The eight stamps must feel like a deliberately designed collectible series. 3:2 overall composition.
```

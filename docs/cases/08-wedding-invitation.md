**Reconstruction prompt**

```prompt
Create an extremely luxurious editorial flat-lay photograph of an old-world European wedding invitation.
Center the composition around a vertically oriented handmade ivory cotton-paper invitation with softly deckled edges and antique gold engraving / letterpress illustration.
At the top: an elaborate European heraldic crest with a crown, ornate shield and intertwined initials, flanked by two heraldic lions and classical winged figures.
A botanical and rococo ornamental border of roses, leaves and scrollwork surrounds the invitation.
The invitation text should clearly read:
TOGETHER WITH THEIR FAMILIES
Amelia Rose & Ethan James
REQUEST THE HONOR OF YOUR PRESENCE AT THEIR MARRIAGE
SATURDAY, THE FOURTEENTH OF JUNE TWO THOUSAND TWENTY-FIVE AT FIVE O'CLOCK IN THE EVENING
Villa Eterna, LAKE COMO, ITALY
DINNER, DANCING, AND A LIFETIME OF LOVE TO FOLLOW
Along the bottom, a delicate antique engraved landscape of a grand Italian villa on Lake Como.
Surrounding flat-lay props: open envelope with a vintage Lake Como illustration, cream-white roses and baby's breath, sage-green and champagne silk ribbons, antique bronze wax seal, ornate gilded picture frames, aged brass objects, a dark green velvet ring box containing a diamond ring.
Warm muted champagne, ivory, bronze, sage and antique-gold palette. Soft directional window light. 2:3 vertical composition.
No modern minimalist design. No bright white digital-paper appearance.
```

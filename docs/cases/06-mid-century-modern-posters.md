```prompt
Create a perfectly aligned 3 x 3 grid containing nine separate vertical mid-century modern motivational posters.
All nine posters belong to the same visual identity system, inspired by optimistic 1960s-1970s graphic design, Swiss modernism, Bauhaus geometry and vintage editorial posters.
Use one consistent limited palette across the collection: warm cream background, cobalt blue, bright orange-red, mustard yellow, forest green, bubblegum pink, and black.
Typography must be large, highly legible and intentionally integrated into each composition.
Poster 1: exact text "Travel Farther" — yellow setting sun above multicolored perspective roads.
Poster 2: exact text "CREATE" — huge rounded blue psychedelic lettering and flowing layered organic waves.
Poster 3: exact text "GROW TOGETHER" — simplified geometric flower.
Poster 4: exact text "LISTEN MORE" — overlapping human face profiles.
Poster 5: exact text "FIND YOUR BALANCE" — stacked circles and semicircles.
Poster 6: exact text "DRINK MORE WATER" — condensed white block lettering on cobalt blue with one orange-red circle.
Poster 7: exact text "LEARN SOMETHING NEW" — surreal staircase into a rounded doorway.
Poster 8: exact text "SEE THE BEAUTY" — black-and-white halftone eye with translucent colorful circles.
Poster 9: exact text "CHOOSE KINDNESS" — geometric blocks with a large pink heart.
Flat printed poster aesthetic, sharp typography, subtle vintage ink texture, no mockup frames, equal gutters, 3:4 overall composition.
```

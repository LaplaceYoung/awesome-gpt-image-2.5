```prompt
A vertical impressionist gouache and thick-oil painting of San Francisco on a bright clear day.
View downhill along a steep residential street toward San Francisco Bay. Pastel-colored houses and dense rounded trees frame both sides of the descending street.
In the distance, the Golden Gate Bridge stretches horizontally across brilliant blue water, painted in vivid vermilion red. Coit Tower appears on the right hillside. Small sailboats float in the bay.
Use a joyful saturated palette: cerulean and cobalt blue sky and water, warm coral, peach, cream, lavender, hot pink bougainvillea, olive and deep green trees, orange rooftops.
Loose confident brushwork, visible thick paint texture, palette-knife marks, simplified architecture, large blocks of color, minimal fine outlines, beautiful broken-color shadows.
Mid-century California plein-air painting feeling, sunny Mediterranean-like atmosphere, optimistic and elegant rather than photorealistic.
```

## P-16 `wedding-invitation.webp`

- Gallery: G-08
- Kind: t2i

```
Create an extremely luxurious editorial flat-lay photograph of an old-world European wedding invitation.
Center the composition around a vertically oriented handmade ivory cotton-paper invitation with softly deckled edges.
The invitation uses extremely intricate antique gold engraving / letterpress illustration.
At the top:
an elaborate European heraldic crest with a crown, ornate shield and elegant intertwined initials, flanked symmetrically by two heraldic lions and classical winged figures.
A highly detailed botanical and rococo ornamental border of roses, leaves, scrollwork and antique engraving surrounds the invitation.
Use elegant high-contrast calligraphy for the couple's names and small refined Roman serif capitals for the supporting copy.
The invitation text should clearly read:
"TOGETHER WITH THEIR FAMILIES"
"Amelia Rose"
"&"
"Ethan James"
"REQUEST THE HONOR OF YOUR PRESENCE
AT THEIR MARRIAGE"
"SATURDAY, THE FOURTEENTH OF JUNE
TWO THOUSAND TWENTY-FIVE
AT FIVE O'CLOCK IN THE EVENING"
"Villa Eterna"
"LAKE COMO, ITALY"
"DINNER, DANCING, AND A LIFETIME
OF LOVE TO FOLLOW"
Along the bottom of the invitation, incorporate a delicate antique engraved landscape of a grand Italian villa on Lake Como surrounded by mountains, cypress trees and water.
Surrounding flat-lay props:
an open envelope containing a vintage Lake Como illustration,
cream-white roses and baby's breath,
sage-green and champagne silk ribbons,
an antique bronze wax seal,
ornate gilded picture frames,
aged brass objects,
a dark green velvet ring box containing a diamond ring.
Warm muted champagne, ivory, bronze, sage and antique-gold color palette.
Soft directional window light,
beautiful fabric shadows,
rich tactile paper fibers,
antique patina,
shallow but sufficient depth of field,
high-end luxury wedding editorial photography,
Italian aristocratic romance,
timeless old-money aesthetic.
2:3 vertical composition.
No modern minimalist design.
No bright white digital-paper appearance.
Everything should look physically printed, engraved and photographed.
```

## P-15 `vintage-national-park-stamps.png`

- Gallery: G-07
- Kind: t2i

```
Create a collectible sheet of eight vintage U.S. national park postage stamps arranged in a perfectly aligned 4-column x 2-row grid on a pure black background.
Each stamp is a vertical rectangle with authentic cream perforated postage edges and subtle aged paper / screen-print texture.
Unified visual style:
1930s-1950s American national park travel poster,
WPA-era illustration,
vintage lithographic printing,
limited but rich colors,
dark ink outlines,
slightly weathered paper,
highly detailed scenic illustration.
Each stamp must have large, clearly readable park name at the top, smaller "NATIONAL PARK" text underneath, state/location in the upper-right, and a narrow black caption strip along the bottom.
Top row:
1. Yellowstone
location: "WY, MT, ID"
illustration: Old Faithful erupting beside turquoise geothermal pools and dark pine forest
bottom caption:
"AMERICA'S FIRST NATIONAL PARK"
2. Grand Canyon
location: "ARIZONA"
illustration: immense layered red-orange canyon with the Colorado River
bottom caption:
"A WORLD OF WONDER"
3. Acadia
location: "MAINE"
illustration: rocky Atlantic shoreline, evergreen trees and a white lighthouse
bottom caption:
"WHERE MOUNTAINS MEET THE SEA"
4. Zion
location: "UTAH"
illustration: monumental orange-red cliffs, green vegetation and a river
bottom caption:
"NATURE'S CATHEDRAL"
Bottom row:
5. Glacier
location: "MONTANA"
illustration: dramatic snow-covered mountains, pine forest and turquoise alpine lake
bottom caption:
"CROWN OF THE CONTINENT"
6. Great Smoky Mountains
location:
"TENNESSEE / NORTH CAROLINA"
illustration: layered misty blue-green mountains, dense forest and foreground wildflowers
bottom caption:
"FOREVER WILD"
7. Denali
location: "ALASKA"
illustration: enormous snowy Denali peak with evergreen forest and a caribou in the foreground
bottom caption:
"THE GREAT ONE"
8. Everglades
location: "FLORIDA"
illustration: sunset wetland, cypress trees, lily pads and a great blue heron
bottom caption:
"RIVER OF GRASS"
The eight stamps must feel like a deliberately designed collectible series, not eight unrelated images.
3:2 overall composition.
Highly legible historically inspired typography.
```

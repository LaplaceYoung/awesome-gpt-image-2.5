## P-09 `mid-century-modern-posters.png`

- Gallery: G-06
- Kind: t2i

```
Create a perfectly aligned 3 x 3 grid containing nine separate vertical mid-century modern motivational posters.
All nine posters belong to the same visual identity system, inspired by optimistic 1960s-1970s graphic design, Swiss modernism, Bauhaus geometry and vintage editorial posters.
Use one consistent limited palette across the collection:
warm cream background,
cobalt blue,
bright orange-red,
mustard yellow,
forest green,
bubblegum pink,
and black.
Use bold geometric shapes, oversized circles, arches, stylized flowers, optical forms, simple silhouettes, clean flat vector-like areas and occasional halftone photographic collage.
Typography must be large, highly legible and intentionally integrated into each composition.
Poster 1:
exact text "Travel Farther"
large black bold type,
a yellow setting sun above multicolored perspective roads converging toward the horizon.
Poster 2:
exact text "CREATE"
huge rounded blue psychedelic lettering,
flowing layered organic waves in pink, orange, yellow, green, blue and black.
Poster 3:
exact text "GROW TOGETHER"
bold black type,
a highly simplified geometric flower with orange circular blossom, pink petals and green/black leaves.
Poster 4:
exact text "LISTEN MORE"
multiple overlapping human face profiles in black, blue, orange and cream.
Poster 5:
exact text "FIND YOUR BALANCE"
stacked and overlapping circles and semicircles creating an abstract balance composition.
Poster 6:
exact text "DRINK MORE WATER"
enormous condensed white block lettering on a cobalt-blue background with one orange-red circle.
Poster 7:
exact text "LEARN SOMETHING NEW"
a surreal staircase leading upward into a rounded blue-and-black doorway.
Poster 8:
exact text "SEE THE BEAUTY"
a black-and-white halftone human eye layered with translucent colorful circles.
Poster 9:
exact text "CHOOSE KINDNESS"
playful abstract geometric blocks with a large pink heart in the center.
Flat printed poster aesthetic,
sharp typography,
subtle vintage ink texture,
no mockup frames,
no perspective distortion,
equal gutters,
clean gallery-sheet presentation,
3:4 overall composition.
```
